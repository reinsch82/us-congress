About the data

The data comes from legislators-historical.yaml and legislators-current.yaml on the repository's main branch. A daily build saves a trimmed copy with this page. When GitHub has newer data, a link at the top loads it live, and if the copy can't be loaded, the page fetches the live data automatically. Seats are grouped by state and Senate class or House district. A seat counts as part of a Congress if anyone held it during that Congress, and it shows as vacant on days nobody held it.

Before about 1989, House records (and many early Senate records) give a full-Congress span for every member, even when a member died or resigned partway through. When a successor's start date is recorded, the predecessor's service is cut off on that day. When it isn't, the seat shows one member and the tooltip names the others who also held it during that Congress. Members whose service began when the first session convened are counted from the day the Congress began. Before 1840, “Republican” in the data means the Jeffersonian Democratic-Republicans.

Keyboard: ← → step through changes, PageUp/PageDown through Congresses, Space plays. Click a seat to see everyone who has held it.
