About the data

Who held each state's governor office, and when, comes from Wikidata. Some governors are in Wikidata without dates, and some terms are missing. Those gaps are filled with years in office from the National Governors Association, via Jacob Kaplan's dataset (until 2020). Such terms only have a year, so they are shown as starting and ending on January 1 and marked “year only”. A few gaps remain where neither source has a governor; the map shows those states as “no governor recorded”. Today's governors are checked against Open States before each update.

A governor with several parties in Wikidata is assigned the party recorded for that time, or else the one recorded by the National Governors Association, or else the one that existed then. Territorial and colonial governors are not included. Corrections made in Wikidata show up here the next day.

Keyboard: ← → step through changes, PageUp/PageDown through years, Space plays. Click a state to see all its governors.
